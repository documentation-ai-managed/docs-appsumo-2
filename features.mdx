## Searching and Filtering Deals

Navigate thousands of deals effortlessly. Use the search bar to query terms like "email marketing" or "SEO tools". Refine results with filters for categories, price ranges, and deal types.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Basic Search" icon="search">
    Enter keywords directly.

    ```
    appsumo.com/search?q=email+automation
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Advanced Filters" icon="filter">
    Apply multiple criteria simultaneously.

    Steps to filter:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Category">
        Choose from Productivity, Marketing, or Development.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set Price Range">
        Filter deals under `{< $50}` or `{< $100}`.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sort by Popularity">
        View top-rated or newest deals first.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Combine filters for precise results, such as "AI tools under `{< $49}` with 4.5+ stars".
</Callout>

## Understanding Lifetime Deals

Lifetime deals grant perpetual access for a one-time payment, eliminating subscriptions. Verify deal terms like update policies and user limits before purchase.

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| One-time fee | No monthly costs |
| Full features | Access all tools forever |
| Limited spots | Act fast before sold out |

<Expandable title="Deal Anatomy" default-open="true">
  Typical structure: Base plan + upgrades. Example: `{10 users}` included, add `{50 users}` for extra.
</Expandable>
