## Steps to Redeem

After purchase, you’ll see your unique code in the "Products" section of your AppSumo account.

1. Sign in to your AppSumo account.
2. Go to "Products" > find your deal.
3. Click "Redeem" or view your purchased codes.
4. Copy the code provided.
5. Visit the partner’s redemption page (link provided).
6. Enter your code and follow partner instructions.

<Callout kind="info">
Check redemption instructions per deal, as each partner may differ.
</Callout>

Need more help? Contact support@appsumo.com.
