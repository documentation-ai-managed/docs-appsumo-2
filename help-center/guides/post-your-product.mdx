## Launch Your Deal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply as a partner">
    ````
    Go to https://partners.appsumo.com and click “Apply Now.”
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete application">
    ````
    Fill out details about your product, value, and features.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and wait for review">
    ````
    AppSumo’s team will contact you with next steps if your product is a good fit.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">Promote your listing in the Sumo-ling Community for more exposure!</Callout>
