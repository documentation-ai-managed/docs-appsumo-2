## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">
Before starting, ensure you have:
- A valid email address
- A web browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Safari recommended)
- Payment method ready (credit card or PayPal)
</Callout>

AppSumo makes it easy to discover lifetime deals on productivity tools. Follow these steps to create an account, find your first deal, and complete a purchase.

## Step 1: Create a Free Account

Sign up takes under 2 minutes. Use the official AppSumo website.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit AppSumo" icon="globe">
    Open your browser and go to [https://appsumo.com](https://appsumo.com).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sign Up" icon="user-plus">
    Locate the "Sign Up" button in the top-right corner and click it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details" icon="edit-3">
    Provide your email, create a password, and agree to the terms. Verify your email via the confirmation link sent to your inbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Browse Curated Deals

AppSumo curates deals across categories like marketing, SEO, and productivity.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Featured Deals" icon="star">
    On the homepage, scroll to the "Featured" section. Deals are sorted by popularity and expiration date.
    
    Look for badges like `<90% off` or "Limited Time".
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Categories" icon="list">
    Click "Browse All Deals" in the navigation menu. Filter by categories such as:
    
    | Category     | Example Tools                  |
    |--------------|--------------------------------|
    | Marketing    | Email tools, ad managers       |
    | Productivity | Task managers, automation apps |
    | SEO          | Keyword research, rank trackers|
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
Use the search bar at the top to find specific tools, like "email marketing".
</Callout>

## Step 3: Make Your First Purchase

Once you find a deal, purchasing is straightforward.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Deal" icon="shopping-cart">
    Click "Get Deal" on your chosen product page. Review features, pricing tiers, and terms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Tier" icon="package">
    Pick the stack size (e.g., 1 code for solo use). Add to cart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Checkout" icon="credit-card">
    Proceed to checkout. Enter payment details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Credit Card" icon="credit-card">
    Enter card number, expiry, and CVV. AppSumo uses secure processing.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="PayPal" icon="paypal">
    Select PayPal and log in to complete payment instantly.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Step 4: Access Purchase History

Track all your deals and licenses post-purchase.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Account" icon="user">
    Click your profile icon in the top-right and select "Purchases".
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Details" icon="file-text">
    Find your deal in the list. Click it to access download links, license keys, and renewal info.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage Subscriptions" icon="settings">
    Use the dashboard to gift codes, request refunds (within policy), or view support docs.
  </Step>
</Steps>
