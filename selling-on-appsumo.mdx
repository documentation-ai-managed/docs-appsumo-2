## Applying as a Vendor

Start by submitting your application through the vendor portal. AppSumo reviews products based on innovation, market fit, and deal potential.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Account" icon="user-plus">
    Visit `https://appsumo.com/vendors` and sign up with your email.

    ```

    bash
    # Example signup curl (for reference)
    curl -X POST https://appsumo.com/api/vendors/signup \
      -d 'email=your@email.com&password=securepass123'
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Application" icon="file-text">
    Fill out the form with product details: name, URL, target audience, and unique value proposition.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Product Demo" icon="play-circle">
    Schedule a 30-minute call. Prepare a live demo showcasing core features.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approval Wait" icon="clock">
    Hear back in 3-7 days. Approved vendors get access to the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Highlight what makes your tool stand out, like AI-powered automation or no-code integrations.
</Callout>

## Preparing Product Listings

Craft compelling listings with screenshots, videos, and feature breakdowns. Use high-quality assets to convert browsers into buyers.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Essential Elements" icon="list">
    Include a hero image, 5-7 feature bullets, and tiered pricing table.

    | Element       | Description                  | Required |
    |---------------|------------------------------|----------|
    | Hero Video   | 60-second overview          | Yes     |
    | Screenshots  | 5+ high-res images          | Yes     |
    | Changelog    | Update history              | Yes     |
    | Refund Policy| Clear terms                 | Yes     |
  </Tab>

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Setting Deal Terms and Pricing

Define your deal structure: lifetime access tiers, discounts, and minimum sales goals. AppSumo takes 50-70% revenue share based on volume.

<CodeGroup tabs="Basic,Advanced">
  ```json
  {
    "dealName": "Lifetime Access - Basic",
    "price": 49,
    "features": ["Core tools", "1 user"],
    "minSales": 500
  }
  ```
  ```json
  {
    "dealName": "Lifetime Access - Pro",
    "price": 99,
    "features": ["All tools", "5 users", "Custom API"],
    "minSales": 1000,
    "upsellPath": "/dashboard/upgrade"
  }
  ```
</CodeGroup>

<Callout kind="alert">
  Set realistic pricing: $39-$199 tiers perform best. Avoid >70% discounts to maintain perceived value.
</Callout>

## Managing Customer Support and Updates

Use AppSumo's portal for ticket management and announcements. Respond within 24 hours and post monthly updates.

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Support Best Practices" default-open="true">
    - Segment queries: bugs, features, billing.
    - Use templates for common issues.
    - Escalate to AppSumo for fraud.

    ```

    markdown
    **Support Ticket Template**

    Hi {customer_name},

    Thanks for reaching out. We're investigating your {issue_type}.

    Expected resolution: {timeline}

    Best,
    Your Team
    ```
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Update Cadence">
    Announce via portal: quarterly major updates, monthly patches. Track via changelog.md.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
